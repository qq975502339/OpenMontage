# Rewrite Rubric

Use this to review a topic, outline, draft, or rewritten script. Score only when useful; otherwise use the categories as a diagnostic checklist.

## 100-Point Rubric

- Core question clarity: 15
  - 0-5: topic label only
  - 6-10: clear question but weak tension
  - 11-15: sharp causal puzzle with audience promise
- Hidden variable strength: 15
  - 0-5: no central variable
  - 6-10: plausible but underused
  - 11-15: explains most sections and creates momentum
- Mechanism chain: 20
  - 0-7: list of causes
  - 8-14: causal order exists but skips steps
  - 15-20: decisions, incentives, and consequences are explicit
- Narrative flow: 15
  - 0-5: facts are piled up
  - 6-10: sections are organized
  - 11-15: each section creates the next question
- Cognitive load control: 10
  - 0-3: too many terms and names
  - 4-7: mostly clear with some heavy patches
  - 8-10: complex ideas arrive only when needed
- Evidence discipline: 10
  - 0-3: unsupported claims
  - 4-7: some evidence, weak labeling
  - 8-10: data/example/inference are separated
- Spoken Chinese readability: 10
  - 0-3: written-essay tone
  - 4-7: readable but stiff
  - 8-10: conversational and precise
- Ending reversal: 5
  - 0-2: summary only
  - 3-4: implication exists
  - 5: ending changes how the opening is understood

## Review Priority Order

1. Fix the core question.
2. Fix the hidden variable.
3. Fix the mechanism chain.
4. Cut or relocate facts.
5. Polish voice and rhythm.
